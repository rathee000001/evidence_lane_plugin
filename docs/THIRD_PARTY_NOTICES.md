# Third-party notices

Evidence Lane depends on open-source projects, runtimes, models, fonts, assets, specifications, and external services that retain their own licenses and terms.

## Authoritative records

The detailed package notice is [plugins/evidence-lane-plugin/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../plugins/evidence-lane-plugin/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Toolchain license records live with their owning packages under `plugins/evidence-lane-plugin/toolchains/licenses/` and the release component manifests.

Those records, package lockfiles, installed-distribution metadata, and bundled license files are authoritative. This overview does not replace them.

## Product and website foundations

The public frontend uses projects including Next.js, React, TypeScript, Three.js, Framer Motion, and their dependencies. Studio uses React, TypeScript, Vite, and the local Windows application/toolchain components declared by its release.

## Data, document, and media tooling

Operation-specific routes may use Python, Node.js, SQLite, pandas, PyArrow, Polars, DuckDB, SQLAlchemy, document and presentation libraries, PDF/OCR tools, OpenCV, FFmpeg, LibreOffice, and other declared packages. Presence in the catalogue does not grant a license or prove readiness.

## Retrieval, workflow, and observability tooling

Declared routes include MCP SDKs, FastMCP, Pydantic, FastAPI, LangGraph, LangChain, LlamaIndex, vector/index libraries, evaluation tools, and observability adapters. External services retain their own accounts, terms, credentials, and data practices.

## Models and providers

Model files and provider runtimes retain their own licenses and use restrictions. Hardware support, model presence, and operation eligibility are separately measured. Ghostscript remains explicitly license-gated.
